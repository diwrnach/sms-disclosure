Household Assistant SMS Program
Disclosure & Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

About This Program

This SMS program delivers personal and household notifications from a private, personal-use automated assistant. Messages include items such as household reminders, weekly meal plans, schedule notifications, and system status alerts. This is a personal, non-commercial program. No marketing, promotional, or solicitation messages are sent.

Opt-In

Recipients are limited to the program owner and household members or family who have given the program owner their direct, express consent to receive messages. Phone numbers are added manually by the program owner only after consent is given. Numbers are never collected from websites, purchased lists, or third parties.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. Typical volume is a few messages per week. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time. You will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact the program owner directly.

Privacy

Phone numbers and message content are used solely to deliver the notifications described above. We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose phone numbers or message content to any third party for marketing or any other purpose. Messages are transmitted through Twilio, our SMS service provider, solely for delivery. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Contact

Questions about this program may be directed to the program owner via the contact method provided when you opted in.

This is a private, personal-use messaging program and is not affiliated with any business, product, or service.
