Privacy Policy

Household Assistant SMS Program
Last updated: September 17, 2026

Information We Collect

This program collects only the phone numbers of consenting recipients and the content of messages sent and received. Phone numbers are added manually by the program owner only after the recipient has given direct, express consent. Numbers are never collected through websites, purchased lists, or third parties.

How Information Is Used

Phone numbers and message content are used solely to deliver the personal household notifications described in our Terms and Conditions. This is a private, non-commercial program. No marketing, advertising, or promotional messages are sent.

Information Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Messages are transmitted through Twilio, our SMS service provider, solely for the purpose of message delivery. We do not sell, rent, or trade phone numbers or message content with any party for any purpose.

Opt-Out

Recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by informing the program owner directly. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data Retention

Phone numbers are retained only while a recipient remains opted in. Upon opt-out, no further messages are sent to that number.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to the program owner via the contact method provided at the time of opt-in.

This is a private, personal-use messaging program and is not affiliated with any business, product, or service. See also: Terms and Conditions.
