Program Description

The Household Assistant SMS Program is a private, personal-use notification service. Enrolled recipients receive personal and household notifications, including household reminders, weekly meal plans, schedule notifications, and system status alerts. This is a non-commercial program. No marketing, advertising, or promotional content is sent.

Opt-In

Recipients are limited to the program owner and household members or family who give the program owner their direct, express verbal consent to receive messages. At the time of consent, recipients are informed that they will receive recurring automated personal notifications, that message frequency varies, that message and data rates may apply, and that they may opt out at any time by replying STOP or ask for assistance by replying HELP.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. Typical volume is a few messages per week. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out and Help

You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP. After you send STOP, you will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent. To rejoin, reply START or inform the program owner. For assistance, reply HELP or contact the program owner directly.
